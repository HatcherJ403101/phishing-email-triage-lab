# Phishing Email Investigations

This folder contains structured investigations of suspicious email samples for a junior Security Operations Centre (SOC) portfolio.

Each investigation aims to document the email's characteristics, identify suspicious indicators, assess the available evidence, and recommend appropriate defensive actions.

## Investigation Method

- Review the sender, subject and message content.
- Examine available email headers and authentication results.
- Identify suspicious URLs, domains and attachment references without opening them.
- Record observable evidence and distinguish it from assumptions.
- Assess the risk and document recommended SOC response actions.

## Safety and Scope

Investigations use samples for educational and defensive purposes. Links are not visited, attachments are not executed, and findings are based only on the evidence available in each sample.
